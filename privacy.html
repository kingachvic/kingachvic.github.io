Privacy policy

Effective 15 November 2026

MiC Study ("the app") is a study app made by Victor King’au, an individual developer. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. In short: your study data stays on your device and, if you sign in, in your own Google Drive. The developer runs no server and receives none of it.

1. Data you put in the app

The app stores what you add to it: library files (PDFs, slides, documents, YouTube links), notes and handwriting, highlights and pen marks, flashcards and your answers to them, topics, revision schedules, exams and your study log. It also stores your settings.

All of this is saved on your device. In the Android app it is in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read and which is deleted when you uninstall the app. In the browser version it is in your browser's storage for this site, which other sites can't read and which is deleted when you clear this site's data.

2. Your Google account and Google Drive

Signing in with Google is optional. The app works fully without it, and you can sign out at any time in Settings → Sync.

If you sign in, the app requests a single permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file: "See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app." It cannot see, open or list any other file in your Drive. It does not ask for your contacts, Gmail, calendar or any other Google data.

The app uses this permission only to:

  • create a folder named MiC Study in your Drive and keep a copy of your app data and library files in it, so your devices stay in sync;
  • read and update those files when you use the app on another device;
  • convert a PowerPoint file you add into a PDF, by uploading it to your Drive as Google Slides and exporting it as a PDF.

Google gives the app your account's email address when you sign in. It is kept on your device only, to show which account is connected.

The connection to Google is made directly from your device over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. The access tokens Google issues stay on your device. The browser version loads Google's own sign-in script from accounts.google.com when you sign in or are signed in.

Limited Use

MiC Study's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, data from your Google account is used only to provide the sync and conversion features described above. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with anyone, not read by the developer, and not used to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

3. What the developer receives

Nothing. The app has no server of its own, no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer can't see your files, notes or activity.

If you send feedback or report a problem, the developer receives only what you choose to write in that message.

4. Other services the app connects to

  • YouTube. When you add or play a YouTube video, the app loads it with YouTube's official embedded player and shows its thumbnail from YouTube. YouTube and Google receive what any embedded video receives, under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • AI assistants. If you choose an assistant in Settings (such as Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini), the card-making buttons copy a prompt to your clipboard and open that assistant's website or app. The app doesn't send anything to the assistant itself: what you paste there is up to you, under that service's own policy.
  • Notifications (study reminders, Pomodoro timer) are scheduled on your device and don't pass through any server.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your device: delete items in the app. To remove everything stored on a device, uninstall the Android app, or clear this site's data in your browser.
  • In Google Drive: delete the MiC Study folder from your Drive to remove the synced copy.
  • Revoke access: sign out in the app, and remove MiC Study from your Google account's third-party connections at any time.

Because the developer holds no copy, there is nothing further to request deletion of. You're welcome to ask if you have questions.

6. Security

Data on your device is kept in the app's private storage. Data sent to Google Drive travels over HTTPS and is stored under your Google account's protections. Keep your device locked and your Google account secured with a strong password and two-step verification.

7. Children

MiC Study is made for university and medical students. It is not directed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly handle children's data.

8. Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above changed. A change that affects your Google data will be shown in the app before it takes effect.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: open an issue on the MiC Study site.
